O&M PropertyGuidelines01 / 21
Introduction

Why imagery carries this brand

O&M's identity is quiet. Hairline marks, a restrained palette and generous space. A system like this does not decorate photography, it depends on it. Every frame either proves the promise of care or undoes it.
The brand asks for restraint
Warm, low-lit, composed imagery. The identity flatters excellent photography and exposes average photography. There is nothing loud to hide behind.
The market asks for difference
The corridor's franchises shoot high-volume and high-saturation. The prestige set never comes north. Honest, quality-first imagery is unclaimed ground.
The law asks for honesty
Australian Consumer Law treats materially altered imagery as misleading conduct. Penalties reach seven figures for agencies. Our standard sits comfortably above the legal line.
One brand, not five photographers
A fixed white balance, contrast treatment and compositional language across every shoot, whoever holds the camera.
O&M PropertyPhotography Guidelines02 / 21
The Policy

The honest imagery policy

Honesty without spin is a value, so it is also an editing rule. This is the line between presenting a home at its best and presenting a home that does not exist.
Approved

We do

  • Declutter physically before the shoot, never digitally erase built features
  • Balance natural and artificial light
  • Straighten verticals and correct lens distortion
  • Day-to-dusk conversion, disclosed on request
  • Virtual staging of empty rooms, always labelled
Never

We never do

  • Alter surfaces, paint, lawns or views
  • Remove defects, poles, wires that affect value
  • Oversaturate grass or sky beyond natural
  • Use distorting ultra-wide angles to inflate rooms
  • Show renovations that have not happened
Always

We disclose

  • Virtually staged images carry the line “Virtually staged, furniture not included”
  • Boundary overlays on aerials marked approximate
  • Renders labelled as artist impressions

Why it matters: ACL penalties for misleading imagery reach $1.1M for companies. More importantly, buyers who feel misled at the door never become clients for life.

Standards

Coverage and image counts

Counts follow the supplier agreement: a single 18-image tier for standard listings, so premium framing never depends on paying for extra frames.
Standard listing · 18 images
Facade hero, street context, every key living zone, kitchen, main suite, bathrooms, garden, and two detail frames. Plus a measured 2D floorplan.
Lifestyle and acreage · 25+
Adds drone coverage, land context, outbuildings, and corridor lifestyle frames. Full-bleed board crops composed on site, not found later.
Apartments and units · 12
Tighter set. Living, kitchen, bedrooms, bathroom, balcony outlook, building entry, one amenity frame.
Leased management stock · 10
Honest, bright, current. Rentals are marketed with the same care as sales. Treated well today, buying tomorrow.
Every shoot delivers
Print and web resolution, full-res unwatermarked originals, unrestricted licence to O&M. Turnaround within 48 hours, re-shoot policy in writing.
O&M PropertyPhotography Guidelines04 / 21
Preparation

Preparing the home

The vendor checklist goes out with the booking confirmation, one week before the shoot. A prepared home saves an hour on site and a thousand dollars in retouching we refuse to do anyway.

Inside

  • Declutter benches, fridges, floors
  • Beds made, linen pressed, towels fresh
  • All globes working, blinds open
  • Personal photos and valuables away
  • Pets and bowls out of frame

Outside

  • Lawns cut, edges done, hoses coiled
  • Cars off the driveway, bins out of sight
  • Pool cleaned, cover off
  • Outdoor furniture set, cushions on

On the day

  • Photographer arrives to a ready home, styled in neutral, minimal tones
  • Vendor walk-through of the three features they love most, those get the extra frames
  • Agent present for the first shoot with any new photographer
O&M PropertyPhotography Guidelines05 / 21
Externals

Externals, day and dusk

Golden hour is the default for hero frames, not the exception. Midday sun produces hard shadows and blown highlights that fight a restrained palette.
Approved

Day externals

  • Facades square-on or gently angled, verticals true
  • Sky natural blue with cloud ratio under 20%
  • Grass deep and natural, never cyan-shifted
  • Sensor and sensor lights on, blinds open
  • Room in frame for board and print crops
Hero use

Dusk externals

  • Reserved for hero listings and full-bleed boards
  • Warm interior glow, every light on
  • Blue-hour gradient sky, consistent between images
  • One dusk hero per campaign, not a dusk gallery
Never

Avoid

  • Power lines, bins, cars and street signs dominating frame
  • Non-symmetric angles that bend the house
  • Skies that are flat, black or obviously replaced
  • Too much driveway in the foreground
O&M PropertyPhotography Guidelines06 / 21
Internals

Internals

Wide enough to describe the room, honest enough to survive the first inspection. If a buyer walks in and feels the room is smaller than the photo, the photo failed.

Camera

  • 16 to 24mm full-frame equivalent, nothing wider
  • Height between 1.2m and 1.5m, level
  • One-point or gentle two-point perspective
  • Verticals corrected, never converging

Light

  • Natural light first, ambient balanced with subtle flash
  • Warm white balance, fixed across the set
  • Windows readable, not blown white
  • No heavy HDR halos, ever

Composition

  • Two to three frames per key zone
  • Lead with the room's best true feature
  • Styling minimal and neutral
  • Detail frames: joinery, tapware, fireplace, finishes
O&M PropertyPhotography Guidelines07 / 21
Aerial

Aerial and drone

Aerials do the local-knowledge work: position, boundary, and what the street offers. They are proof of place, not decoration.
What aerials must show
Position relative to schools, shops, reserves and transport. The corridor context that a facade shot cannot carry.
Boundary overlays
Hairline dashed strokes in ivory, marked approximate, never implying a survey. One overlay frame per campaign.
Compliance
CASA rules apply: licensed operator (RePL) or a ReOC business for drones over 2kg, 120m ceiling, 30m from people, no-fly zones respected. Neighbouring homes framed incidentally, never featured.
Altitude discipline
Two heights only: elevated facade (8 to 15m) and context (60 to 90m). The in-between heights flatter nobody's roof.
O&M PropertyPhotography Guidelines08 / 21
Post-production

Editing and grading

The grade is the brand. One treatment, applied identically by every supplier, so the feed reads as one agency rather than five photographers.
Standard

The O&M grade

  • Warm neutral white balance
  • Contrast lifted gently, blacks never crushed
  • Greens natural, skies gradient blue
  • Sharpening applied once, for output size

File discipline

  • Naming: street-number-street-name_frame-number
  • Print res (300dpi) and web res (2048px) sets
  • Full-res originals delivered unwatermarked
  • Colour space sRGB for portals, Adobe RGB for print
Re-shoot

Rejected automatically

  • HDR halos and ghosting
  • Cyan grass, purple skies
  • Warped verticals
  • Visible photographer or drone reflections
  • Inconsistent grade within a set
O&M PropertyPhotography Guidelines09 / 21
Watermark

The watermark

Rules carried directly from the identity guidelines. The watermark is a signature, not a security device.
Which mark
Boxed monogram for imagery. 100% opacity always, no semi-transparent versions of the mark itself.
Placement
Bottom corner, clear of faces, house numbers and the primary architectural subject. Never tiled, rotated or centred.
The scrim rule
Where the placement zone averages above 65% luminance, a black 20% scrim sits behind the ivory mark. Mandatory, not optional.
Minimum sizes
Follow the identity minimums: boxed monogram never below 100px on screen or 15mm in print.
O&M PropertyPhotography Guidelines10 / 21
Part Two
Videography
Three jobs: listing films that sell homes, suburb films that build the community hub, and team films that prove the people. Every second in the calm and steady voice.
O&M PropertyVideography Guidelines11 / 21
Listing films

Listing film style

Composed confidence in motion. The steadier the film, the safer the buyer feels. Nothing in an O&M film shouts.
Approved

Pacing

  • Slow, deliberate movement: glides, not whips
  • No speed ramps, no fast cutting
  • 60 to 90 seconds for a listing film
  • Natural sound under tasteful, calm music

People

  • Agents welcome on camera, speaking plainly
  • One person, one message per scene
  • Written in the tone of voice: facts first, no hype
  • Renters and vendors never filmed without consent
Never

Never

  • Letterboxing or gimmick transitions
  • ALL-CAPS supers or exclamation marks
  • Countdown urgency devices
  • Music that sells harder than the home
O&M PropertyVideography Guidelines12 / 21
Structure

Openings, titles and end cards

Identical structure on every film, so the first and last three seconds are unmistakably O&M.
The open
Establishing drone or facade glide. Primary lockup centred, ivory, fading as the movement settles. Same size, same fade, every film.
The headline card
Melodrama headline over charcoal or ivory. Address and beds, baths, cars in Funnel Sans. One card, four seconds.
Colour-coded end cards
Sage for sales. Butter yellow for lease. Chocolate for off-market. The palette's service-line roles, applied in motion.
The close
Agent name and mobile in Funnel Sans, then the lockup on charcoal. No calls to action, the number is the call to action.
O&M PropertyVideography Guidelines13 / 21
Specifications

Capture and delivery

Locked from the supplier rebrief: both orientations from every session, and the raw clips stay with O&M.
Capture
4K minimum, log profile where available, stabilised. Golden hour exteriors wherever scheduling allows.
Deliver
16:9 master and 9:16 vertical cut from the same shoot. Vertical is where property video is actually watched.
Raw clips
Stabilised raw clips delivered alongside the edit. They become website headers, social tiles and board imagery for a year, at no extra cost.
Captions
Subtitled by default for social. Written in the voice: under 20 words a line, no hype.
Suburb films
Evergreen 60-second pieces on Botanic Ridge, Somerville, Pearcedale. Real locals, named places, golden hour. The content engine behind the community hub.

Part Three
The team & place shoot
A detailed brief for the headshot and office photography. One photographer, one day, split across two half-day sessions, so people and place carry one lighting sensibility.
O&M PropertyShoot Brief15 / 21
Overview

The brief at a glance

The team is the brand. Full names carry the website for year one, and every face on a board, card or profile comes from this shoot.
Who
Brooke Mitchell, Jamie O'Halloran, Chloe Watts, and team. Photographer: Grace (availability being confirmed), Driftwood Studio as alternate.
Where
Session one at Sam's location, as agreed. Session two at the new office, once the fit-out completes next year.
When
Session one on a Thursday, before the end of October. All imagery needed for the November brand-ready deadline.
The one rule
Shoot after the identity is locked. Wardrobe, backdrop and props reflect the final palette, nothing Harcourts-blue in frame.
Budget frame
Full-day rate split across two half-day sessions, per the supplier review: $1,200 to $2,000 all-in, confirmed on quote.
O&M PropertyShoot Brief16 / 21
Session One

Session one · Headshots

Environmental and warm, not studio-stiff. These portraits sit beside the hairline serif on ivory, so they need air, softness and honesty.

Style

  • Natural light, shallow depth, warm neutral grade
  • Backgrounds: ivory wall, timber, garden greenery, soft and unbranded
  • Expressions: at ease, direct, no crossed-arm power poses

Coverage per person

  • Straight-to-camera portrait
  • Working candid, phone or doorway
  • Walking exterior frame
  • Three crops from each: 1:1 social, 4:5 web, 3:2 print
  • Matched eye-line and scale across the team

Wardrobe

  • Plain, tonal: ivory, slate, charcoal, chocolate
  • No busy patterns, no visible logos, pressed
  • Two outfits each, one formal, one relaxed
  • Makeup matte and natural, no gloss
O&M PropertyShoot Brief17 / 21
Session One

Session one · Social and digital

The second half-day feeds the launch content engine: website headers, social templates and the vertical video that introduces the brand.

Stills

  • Team trio and pair configurations
  • At the door, at the board, mid-conversation
  • Corridor b-roll: Settlers Run streets, Botanic Ridge reserve, Somerville shopfronts
  • Landscape and vertical of every setup

Motion

  • Vertical intro clips, one per person, lines written in the voice
  • “We'll call you Tuesday” proof moments, phone in hand
  • Walking-and-talking b-roll for reels
  • Captured 4K, delivered 9:16 and 16:9
Hold

For later use

  • Frames composed with clear space for the boxed monogram
  • Neutral backgrounds where headline type will sit
  • Nothing published before February. Shot now, staged for launch
O&M PropertyShoot Brief18 / 21
Session Two

Session two · Office and place

Held until the fit-out completes next year, shot by the same photographer with the same grade, so people and place read as one brand.
Exterior
Signage hero at dusk, the butter-lit window glow. Street context frames showing position.
Interior
Reception, meeting room, workspace details. Real desks, honest tidiness, no staged laptops-and-lattes.
Details
The embossed cards on the desk, the appraisal folder, the signage type. The brand living in the space.
People in place
Team at work in the finished office, re-matching session one's angles so the website can update without re-shooting faces.
O&M PropertyShoot Brief19 / 21
Logistics

Logistics and delivery

Same supplier disciplines as every other shoot: everything licensed, everything on time, everything named properly.
Call sheet
Half-day blocks of 45 minutes per person plus group time. Wardrobe changes scheduled, not improvised.
Retouching
Natural only. Skin stays real, stray hairs and blemishes softened, nothing plastic. Two rounds of selects.
Delivery
Selects within one week. Full-res originals, unwatermarked, unrestricted licence to O&M.
Approval
Each team member approves their own selects before anything is staged for launch. Sam signs off the set against this brief.

Questions, exceptions and sign-off
These guidelines bind every supplier from the first O&M campaign. Exceptions are approved by Sam before the shoot, never after it. Suppliers receive this document with their engagement letter.
