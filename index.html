Audits & Insights

Nobody in the corridor sets the standard of care

The franchises
Ray White, O'Brien. Scale and systems, standardised tone. High-throughput and transactional: Ray White leased ~900 properties in 12 months. O'Brien's Somerville office leased 5.
The dated local
L.Cooper owns “Somerville independent since 1989” and genuine trust, with a dated brand, three agents and about 620 Instagram followers.
The niche
Homes & Acreage owns lifestyle and acreage storytelling, on a thin rent roll (~52 leased). Everyday homes and landlords go under-served.
The prestige set
RT Edgar, Kay & Burton, Marshall White, Buxton. They set the craft benchmark and serve the southern Peninsula. Minimal presence in the corridor.
The open position is bigger than communication. It is relationships managed properly, expectations set clearly and promises kept. Everything that builds a name worth recommending.

Antimony Studio
Audits & Insights

Four audiences, one expectation

Local Movers
Moving up, moving on, always close to home.

Considered, cautious, loyal to agents who earn it. They value honesty, local knowledge and clear communication above all.
Relocators
Ambition meets lifestyle in every move.

Research-heavy and reassurance-seeking. They don't know the area, they buy on trust, not local familiarity.
Landlords
Your portfolio, in trusted local hands.

Communication and accountability above all. Switchers are actively fleeing agents who leave them in the dark.
Renters
Treated well today, buying tomorrow.

Practical, fast-moving, responsive to respect. Today's renter is tomorrow's buyer, and later, a landlord.

Brand Tone

Three principles, each a position on a scale

01 · Straight-talking
Direct without being blunt. Facts first, bad news early, numbers over adjectives.
02 · Warmly local
Neighbourly without being casual. A neighbour with expertise, not an office with a logo.
03 · Calm & steady
Assured without being salesy. The steadier we sound, the safer people feel.
The scales matter more than the labels, they tell every writer what to move away from.
2026O&M Property
Antimony Studio
Brand Tone · Principle 01

Straight-talking

Dimension: Directness
Spin Blunt
“We lead with the fact. We give the number, then the reasoning. We deliver bad news early, and always with a next step.”
Numbers over adjectives
“$1.04M in 19 days,” not “an outstanding result.”
One message per sentence
If it needs a second breath, it is two sentences.
Never hype, never hedge
No “priced to sell.” No “exciting opportunity.” The fact carries the confidence.
2026O&M Property
Antimony Studio
Brand Tone · Principle 02

Warmly local

Dimension: Warmth
Corporate Neighbourly
“We sound like a neighbour with expertise, not an office with a logo. We write the way we talk at an open home.”
Name the place
The street, the school, the park. Proof of local beats claims of local.
Always human
First-person plural: “we'll walk you through it.”
No franchise-speak
Never “we are pleased to announce.” No borrowed prestige.
2026O&M Property
Antimony Studio
Brand Tone · Principle 03

Calm & steady

Dimension: Energy
Excitable Rehearsed
“Composed confidence. The steadier we sound, the safer people feel, especially when the stakes are high.”
Short when it matters
Short sentences when emotions are big.
Nothing shouted
No exclamation marks, no ALL CAPS, no countdown urgency.
Reassure with process
Say what happens next, not how excited we are.
2026O&M Property
Antimony Studio
Brand Tone

One voice. Four emphases.

To Local Movers
Lead with streets and honesty, at a considered pace.

“You've probably driven past our boards on Browns Road.”
To Relocators
Orient and reassure. Assume no local knowledge; explain once, clearly.

“Here's what locals know about this pocket.”
To Landlords
Precise, accountable, numbered. Fees, dates, next steps.

“You'll have the condition report Thursday. Here's what it covers.”
To Renters
Fast, fair, respectful, either way, they hear from us.

“Your application is in. You'll hear from us by 5pm tomorrow, either way.”
2026O&M Property
Antimony Studio
Brand Tone

When life is hard, the voice gets quieter.

Discretion is the baseline
The circumstances never appear in marketing. Ever.
Symmetry of information
In a separation: one point of contact, both parties equally informed. Equal information is care.
Plain, practical steps
People under stress need the next step, not the big picture.
No urgency devices
Deadlines stated once, calmly, never leveraged.
Separation, deceased estates and financial stress sit behind a meaningful share of sales. We are not “divorce specialists” and never say otherwise. We are the agency that handles hard moments with care, and word of that travels.

Language System

Words we use. Words we never use.

We say
home · your property · renters · appraisal · “here's where it sits” · named streets · “we'll call you Tuesday” · “either way, you'll hear from us”

In practice:
“Buyers are paying $860k to $890k on your street. Here's where your home sits against the last three sales.”

“We'll call you Tuesday with the open home numbers. Either way, you'll hear from us.”
We never say
priced to sell! · motivated vendor · one not to miss · nestled · boasts · oasis · rare opportunity · “we are pleased to announce” · ALL-CAPS anything · stacked superlatives

What that sounds like:
“PRICED TO SELL! This stunning entertainer boasts a rare opportunity, nestled in a sought-after pocket!”

If the line needs an exclamation mark, the fact is not strong enough yet.
The ban list is enforceable. If a line uses one of these, it gets rewritten, no exceptions for portals or boards.
2026O&M Property
Antimony Studio
Language System

The lines the brand repeats

“Local knowledge you can trust.
Communication you can feel.”
The brand pair. Everywhere, always together.
“The property manager you never have to chase.”
The landlord line. It names the difference: no silence, no chasing, no surprises.
“Street by street.”
The local proof line. Pairs with aerial photography and suburb content.
“Our relationships outlast the campaign.”
The team line. Something we can actually say, and then prove.
Already consistent across the positioning, the applied designs and the content strategy. The system repeats these lines, it does not vary them.
2026O&M Property
Antimony Studio
Language System

Five writing rules

Under 20 words
Sentences under twenty words carry the brand best.
Proof within one sentence
Every claim gets a street, a number or a date beside it.
Explain jargon once
“The Section 32, the legal pack your lawyer checks.” Then plain words.
Write to one person
Never to a market segment.
Say O&M in full at first contact
O&M and O'Brien sound alike out loud. Lead with the full name in key moments so credibility lands on us, not them.
The read-aloud test
If you wouldn't say it at an open home, rewrite it.

The voice across the journey

Discover Warm, local, useful. Suburb films and market notes that give before they ask.
